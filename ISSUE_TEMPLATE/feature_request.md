name: Feature request

title: "[feature] "
labels: ["enhancement"]
---

## What problem are you trying to solve?

Describe the problem from your perspective. What are you trying to do, and where does the current behavior fall short? Avoid jumping to a solution here.

## What do you do today to work around it?

How do you handle this currently? Manual steps, scripts, other tools, copy-pasting?

## Proposed solution

Describe what you would like the project to do. If you have multiple ideas, list them — alternatives help reviewers pick the best fit.

## Who benefits?

Is this a one-off for your workflow, or does it generalize? Concrete examples of other users / use cases strengthen the case.

## Additional Context

Sketches, example output, related issues, links to prior art in other tools, screenshots — anything that helps.
